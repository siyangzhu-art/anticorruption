The initial upload inadvertently contained six disaggregated CPED-derived checking CSVs and the singleton-exclusion worksheet in the cumulative workbook. They have been removed from the final release tree and the publication commit replaced. GitHub may retain caches of previous, now-unreferenced commits; immediate erasure of those caches is not verified. The data owner was notified.
